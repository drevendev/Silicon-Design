# Silicon game UI

Inspect revision, loop/world, contracts, text/assets, accepted changes and capacity/growth. Separate speculation; static work needs no invented flows/saves.

## Compose the play decision

Before composing, find and visually inspect comparable genre/task UI; prefer first-party. An article without that screen is not inspection: replace it or disclose the gap before using product evidence. Distinguish shipped/promotion/concept/unknown provenance. Reuse inspected references for local fixes; transfer grouping/density/action relationships, not skins/features.

Identify input, resolution, viewing distance, scale, safe areas/languages. Choose grid/rows from count/growth, recognition/comparison/sorting. Budget space after chrome/status; fit small fixed assortments without scrolling at base size. Sketch against the reading model before replacing it. Reflow/zoom may scroll; tiny text, clipped/hidden offers or needless pagination do not establish fit.

Across screens share semantic colours, type/numbers, icons, density, chrome and component/state implementations. Explain task exceptions; local fixes need no kit.

Name the dominant object/decision. Let shelf goods lead competing frames. Prefer one enclosing boundary; justify nesting. Align corner actions with headers; choose centring/alignment deliberately. Try shared dividers for uniform rows. Retain useful backgrounds/alignment.

Subtract visible labels/badges/help already conveyed by counts, meters, position or selected controls; retain accessible names, context, legends, deficits/blocked reasons and safeguards. Put effects by the affected choice, distinguish applied/possible effects, resource pools and signed units. Familiar remove/search controls need readable glyphs and usable targets. Report fixed-copy/DOM limits.

Use expressive material/type with readable operational text; inspect symbols/selection at use size and scene/object/control edge, lighting, perspective and material coherence. Extend/remap colours coherently. Trace text/glyph SVG/currentColor, masks/filters, surfaces and crops; dark-on-dark fails despite passing swatches.

## Preserve game truth

Show owned/required amounts and exact payable totals visibly at commitment, not solely in accessible names; secondary precision may use detail. Distinguish zero/unknown/not-applicable; preserve knowledge/unlocks including accessible text. Presentation never changes economy/inventory/progression/simulation. Feedback/motion follows authoritative pending/failed/interrupted/completed state; no duplicate purchases, invented events or repeated-input delays.

## Verify within scope

Exercise normal/adverse states, long translations at minimum supported width, large/live values, capacity/detail. Check supported input, focus/restoration, scaling, non-drag/remapping, motion/audio independence and non-colour critical cues. Check interruption/return/removal and promised cross-surface/reload/restart persistence. Recheck consumers.

Before delivery compare same-state original/result for noise, hierarchy and action clarity; correct within budget. Record removals/justified retentions. QA passes neither finish design nor establish owner acceptance. Report checked/failed/untested, unresolved craft/readability and task-relevant shared defects outside edit scope. Simulations prove neither native input/saves nor comprehension/compliance.
