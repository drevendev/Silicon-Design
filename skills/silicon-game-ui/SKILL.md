# Silicon game UI

Inspect current revision, loop/world, contracts, text/assets, accepted changes and capacity/growth plans; separate speculation. Static work needs no invented flows/saves.

## Compose the play decision

Before composing, independently find and visually inspect comparable genre/task screens, even without a research request. Prefer first-party material; distinguish shipped UI, promotion, concepts and unknown provenance. Reuse suitable inspected references for local corrections; report unavailable inspection. Extract grouping, density and action relationships into this game's language, not foreign skins or features.

Identify input, resolution, viewing distance, UI scale, safe areas and languages. Choose grid/rows from fixed versus growing count, recognition, comparison and sorting needs. Budget usable width/height after chrome/status; fit small fixed assortments together at base size without scrolling. Compare a cheap same-content sketch with the existing composition before replacing its useful reading model. Reflow/zoom may need deliberate scrolling; tiny text, clipped/hidden offers or needless pagination do not establish fit.

For multiple screens, share semantic colours, type/numbers, icons, density, chrome and component/state implementations. Explain task-based exceptions; local fixes need no new kit.

Prioritise the play decision/playfield; group names, state, costs/effects and actions. Simplify framing or use familiar remove/search representations when authorized, retaining names, context, targets, safeguards and useful empty/blocked explanations. Report fixed-copy/DOM limits.

Use expressive material/type with readable operational text; distinguish resource/weather/action symbols and selection at use size. Inspect scene/object/icon/control edge, lighting, perspective and material coherence. Extend colours coherently or remap within scope. Trace actual text/glyph colours, SVG fill/stroke/currentColor, masks/filters, backgrounds and crops; dark-on-dark glyphs fail despite passing swatches.

## Preserve game truth

Show owned/required amounts, costs and blocked reasons; retain consequential exact values or reachable disclosure. Distinguish zero, unknown and not applicable; preserve knowledge/unlock rules including accessible text. Presentation/help must not change economy, inventory, progression or simulation. Feedback follows real pending/failed/interrupted/completed events without duplicate purchases, misleading effects or repeated-input delays; invent no events for personality.

## Verify within scope

Exercise normal/adverse states, long translations, large/live values, capacity and reachable detail in the runtime. Check supported keyboard/controller/pointer/touch, focus/restoration, scaling, non-drag/remapping and motion/audio independence; critical cues need more than colour/sound. Check interruption/return/removal with connected focus/context and promised cross-surface/reload/restart persistence. Recheck consumers; unreadability stays unresolved.

Report contracts and checked/failed/untested coverage, including unavailable runtime. Simulations do not prove native input, saves, comprehension or compliance; assess character separately from operation.
