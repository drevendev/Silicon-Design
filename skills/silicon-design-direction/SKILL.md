# Silicon design direction

Respect scope, brand and useful decisions. A local fix needs one direction.

## Choose the work

Inspect revision, content/assets/components, accepted changes and capacity/growth. Identify person/task/environment/intended feeling; separate requirements/plans/speculation. Ask only about consequential gaps.

Before composing, find and visually inspect comparable category/task UI and provenance. A page without that screen does not count: replace it or disclose the gap before using product evidence. Reuse inspected material for local fixes; transfer relationships, not skins. Choose reading model from task/count; budget space after chrome without tiny text or hidden content.

Express character through subject, vocabulary, output or behaviour; hue/font alone is insufficient. Label supplied/fictional/generated material; inventories are conditional.

## Explore when warranted

For open identity/requested exploration, compare inexpensive same-content/capability/fixture/asset/viewport compositions, including a restrained treatment. Also sketch against the existing composition before replacing a useful reading model. Change dominant object, hierarchy or imagery; retain tradeoffs and develop the selected direction.

Select through dominant object, useful action and next transition. Check narrow/enlarged-text priority and bounded-set fit before polishing. Honour comparison scope; unavailable rendering leaves proposals unverified.

## Carry the choice

Extend the palette coherently or, within authorized redesign, remap surfaces, text, icons and states together. Neither an unrelated accent nor freezing every existing colour follows from respecting identity. No hue is inherently wrong.

Before expanding screens, share visual/component roles: equivalent headers, navigation, levels and controls reuse one contract, with task exceptions. Local fixes need no kit.

Subtract redundant labels/help/frames within scope; retain useful context, state reasons and accessible meaning. Justify nested boundaries competing with the main object. Fixed-copy/DOM limits remain binding. Invent no fields, workflows, persistence or screens. Efficiency can carry identity; expressive detail must not obstruct repeated work.

Compare original/selected composition for noise and hierarchy before delivery; record removals/justified retentions, changed roles and tradeoffs in DESIGN.md when needed. Sheets/states are conditional. Separate proposal/implementation, technical checks, craft judgment and owner acceptance. Invent no audience effects/checks; continue authorized reversible work without another approval sequence.
