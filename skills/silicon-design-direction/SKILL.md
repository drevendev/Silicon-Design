# Silicon design direction

Respect scope, brand constraints and useful decisions. A bounded correction needs one direction, not an identity bundle.

## Choose the work

Inspect current source/revision, content/assets/components, accepted changes and supplied capacity/growth. Identify person, task, environment and intended feeling; distinguish requirements, authorized plans and speculation. Ask only about consequential missing context.

Before composing, independently find and visually inspect comparable category/task references. Identify provenance; record useful relationships and differences, not copied skins. Reuse suitable inspected material for local corrections; report unavailable evidence. Choose the reading model from task/count; budget usable viewport after chrome and retain bounded-set fit without tiny text or hidden content.

Express character through subject, vocabulary, output or behaviour; hue/font/aesthetic alone is insufficient. Label supplied, fictional and generated material; inventories are conditional.

## Explore when warranted

For open identity/requested exploration, compare inexpensive same-content/capability/fixture/asset/viewport compositions, including a restrained treatment. Also sketch against the existing composition before replacing a useful reading model. Change dominant object, hierarchy or imagery; retain tradeoffs and develop the selected direction.

Select through first attention, useful action and next transition. Check narrow/enlarged-text priority before polishing. Honour comparison scope; unavailable rendering leaves proposals unverified.

## Carry the choice

Extend the palette coherently or, within authorized redesign, remap surfaces, text, icons and states together. Neither an unrelated accent nor freezing every existing colour follows from respecting identity. No hue is inherently wrong.

For multiple screens, establish shared visual/component roles before expansion; equivalent headers, navigation, levels and controls reuse one contract, with task-based exceptions. An isolated fix needs no new UI kit.

Permit useful regrouping and removal of redundant presentation when in scope; fixed-copy/DOM restrictions remain binding and must be reported when they prevent the intended result. Do not invent fields, workflows, persistence or screens to demonstrate character. Efficiency can carry identity; expressive detail must not obstruct repeated work.

Record changed hierarchy, roles, rationale/tradeoffs and questions in DESIGN.md. Sheets/states are conditional. Distinguish proposal/prototype/implementation; invent no audience effects or checks. Continue authorized reversible work without another approval sequence.
