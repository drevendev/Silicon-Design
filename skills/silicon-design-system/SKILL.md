# Silicon design system

Reuse the product's current direction, token format and sound components. Before expanding multiple screens, share semantic roles, type/numbers, icons, density, chrome and control/state implementations; explain task-based exceptions. Local corrections need no new kit, framework or specimen bundle.

## Meaning and roles

Test real scripts, long labels, quantities, weights, fallback/loading and licenses. Define reading, UI, display, supporting and numeric roles as needed. Inspect at use size/enlargement; oversized counts must not make important costs or units unreadable.

Extend a palette coherently or remap surface, foreground, icon, action, status, boundary and focus roles together within scope. Do not freeze colours or add unrelated accents. Retain semantic status colour plus text/shape; repair failing variants rather than flattening states to ink.

Measure actual resolved pairs, including inheritance, transparency, overlays and changed themes. Trace SVG fill/stroke/currentColor, masks, filters and image glyphs onto their real surfaces. Check crops, viewBox, padding and overflow at use size. For web text use 4.5:1 ordinary and 3:1 qualifying-large thresholds; meaningful non-text cues use applicable 3:1 requirements/exceptions. Compare unrounded ratios. Selected swatch checks do not verify other glyphs or states.

Unfamiliar codes need a nearby legend and reachable detail through supported pointer, keyboard and touch paths. A detail view can suffice without per-item tooltips. Include truthful success, partial, unknown and blocked examples where relevant.

For charts identify quantity, unit, geography, period, scale and series. Keep visible units by values and labels by marks or clear leaders. Verify source values, totals, denominators, missing data and stacking order. Report contradictions; do not invent repairs. Chart form follows comparison.

## Contracts and checks

Define content/growth limits, spacing/density, responsive/input behaviour and applicable focus, selected, pending, unavailable-with-reason, empty, error and success states. Distinguish zero, unknown and not applicable; retain exact values or reachable exact disclosure when consequential.

Keep tokens authoritative; no unvalidated DTCG claim or duplicate exports. Bound component widths and group actions with objects; compactness preserves necessary text and targets.

Inspect realistic content, long translations, large/live values and affected states across requested consumers. Keep icon meaning and edge/stroke/material coherence. Use sheets only for relevant reusable changes.

Report contracts, measured pairs and checked/failed/untested coverage; scoped readability defects remain unresolved until fixed. Source writes and palette math establish neither rendered quality nor compliance.
