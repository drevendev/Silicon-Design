# Silicon design review

Review revision/direction, constraints and content/growth. State fixture, viewport/scale, theme, language/input; screenshots show appearance only.

Review-only work reports findings. Apply authorized fixes and recheck; routine reversible repairs need no renewed approval.

## Select checks

Inspect dominant object, hierarchy, type/units/status and character. References must show task UI, not article headers; disclose gaps and reading-model/count rationale. Verify bounded-set fit/reflow without tiny text, clipping or needless pagination. Allow coherent palette changes; no hue/font/pattern blacklist.

Trace foregrounds/glyphs, SVG fill/stroke/currentColor, masks/filters and images onto changed surfaces. Inspect bounds/crops/states at use size. Unreadable glyphs fail despite passing swatches; source writes do not prove rendering.

Across screens compare headers/navigation/levels/controls against one shared contract; explain task exceptions. Local fixes need no kit/surface. Check supplied growth, bounded widths, quantities/costs/actions, readable glyphs and usable targets. Exact action totals must be visible at commitment, not only in accessible names. Check long translations at minimum supported width.

Perform a subtraction pass: question every label/badge/help already conveyed by a value, meter, position or selected control. Preserve accessible names, context, legends, empty/blocked reasons and safeguards. Prefer one enclosing boundary per object; justify nested frames or individual card chrome for uniform rows. Inspect alignment, corner actions and object prominence. Fixed-copy/DOM limits never permit fake controls. Inspect landing headline breaks/choices/destinations and chart units/labels/source fidelity.

For controls, transitions, overlays or sticky/scroll regions, read [interactive checks](references/interactive-checks.md); skip for static/screenshot-only work. Full-area activation, deliberate reflow and variable heights are not automatic defects. Check matched content/narrow priority of in-scope alternatives; extra workflows/persistence/sheets remain conditional.

## Report and resolve

Locate findings by state/viewport/region, consequence, correction and evidence/confidence, including task-relevant shared defects outside edit scope. Separate defects, hypotheses and taste. Blocking prevents key task/access/truthful feedback; major harms reading/comprehension/efficiency/identity; polish is smaller craft.

Compare same-state original/result for noise, hierarchy and action clarity. For authorized fixes make a critical correction pass within budget; record removals/justified retentions and recapture/recheck consumers. Review-only work reports corrections. Separate resolved/partial/unresolved from checked/failed/untested; readability defects remain unresolved. No finding quota.

Winners' criticism still matters: QA/preference is not acceptance. Separate input/asset/capture/adapter defects from implementation, source/native input, palette math/rendering, resize/zoom and screenshots/AT. Separate craft from operation/access; audience/compliance/superiority claims need evidence.
