# Silicon design review

Review against the product's task, existing direction and constraints. State the revision, surface, fixture, viewport/scale, theme, language and input paths actually reviewed. A screenshot supports appearance findings only.

For review-only work, report findings without editing. Apply fixes when requested or already authorized, then recheck affected states; routine reversible repairs do not need a new approval sequence.

## Select relevant checks

Inspect the complete requested artifact, content order, hierarchy, necessary type, resolved colour roles, units, status meanings and subject-specific character. Preserve choices that work; a hue, font, library, gradient or card is not a defect by itself.

On operator screens, question redundant framing while retaining useful legends and state explanations. On landings, inspect actual headline breaks, comparison groups and action cues. On charts, inspect nearby units, mark-label relationships and source-data fidelity.

For reviews involving changed controls, state transitions, overlays or sticky/scroll regions, read [interactive checks](references/interactive-checks.md). Skip that reference for screenshot-only or static appearance work. Exercise relevant decisions and recovery where runtime permits; additional tasks, secondary surfaces, persistence tests and sheets are conditional, not automatic deliverables.

Compare retained alternatives only when exploration was in scope, including selected priority after narrow reflow. Keep a product's recognizable idea coherent across the requested states; assess ordinary/repeated use where relevant. Character may live in efficient work, not extra decoration.

## Report and resolve

Each finding needs a located observation/reproduction, task consequence, bounded correction, evidence and confidence. Separate confirmed defects, hypotheses and taste; distinguish geometry, brief interpretation and aesthetic judgment. Blocking prevents a key task/access/truthful feedback; major materially harms comprehension, readability, efficiency or identity; polish is smaller craft work.

For visual corrections, record selector/region, state/viewport, expected change and same-state recapture when possible. Mark resolved/partial/unresolved separately from checked/failed/untested coverage. Do not manufacture a finding quota.

Read winners' comments as well as votes: relative preference is not acceptance. Keep supplied copy defects, unavailable brand assets, capture uncertainty and test-adapter mistakes separate from implementation defects. Do not invent beauty scores, research outcomes or comparative superiority.

Distinguish source assertions from actual activation/focus, palette math from resolved rendering, resize from zoom and screenshots from assistive-technology checks. Report gaps explicitly; static checks do not establish compliance.

Deliver important findings first, evidence, fixes and remaining limits, with separate conclusions about operation/access and character/care. Emotional effects and attachment remain hypotheses without audience observations. Comparative claims require matched artifacts and scoped human evidence; different products alone cannot establish diversity.
