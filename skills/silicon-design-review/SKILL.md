# Silicon design review

Review the requested task against the current revision, direction, constraints and realistic content/growth. State fixture, viewport/scale, theme, language and input actually reviewed. A screenshot supports appearance only.

Review-only work reports findings without editing. Apply authorized fixes and recheck affected states; routine reversible repairs need no new approval sequence.

## Select checks

Inspect the complete artifact: order, hierarchy, necessary type, units, status meaning and subject-specific character. A hue, font or pattern is not inherently a defect. Assess palette coherence, allowing an authorized whole-palette change.

Trace actual foregrounds and glyphs, including SVG fill/stroke/currentColor, masks/filters and image assets, onto changed surfaces. Inspect crop/bounds and relevant states at use size. An unreadable glyph fails despite unrelated passing swatches; source writes do not prove resolved rendering.

For multiple screens, compare equivalent headers, navigation, levels, cards and controls against one shared component contract. Explain task-based exceptions; a local fix needs no kit or extra surface. Inspect supplied growth constraints, bounded row widths and quantities/costs/actions together; compactness must retain reading and targets.

Question redundant framing and missed authorized simplification while preserving useful legends, empty states, accessible meanings and safeguards. Fixed-copy/DOM restrictions are input limits, not permission to fake controls. On landings inspect real headline breaks, comparison and action cues; on charts inspect visible units, mark-label relationships and source fidelity.

For affected controls, transitions, overlays or sticky/scroll regions, read [interactive checks](references/interactive-checks.md). Skip it for static/screenshot-only work. Preserve negative controls: full-area activation, deliberate reflow and content-driven heights are not automatic defects. Compare retained alternatives only when exploration was in scope; check their matched content and narrow priority. Additional workflows, persistence and sheets remain conditional.

## Report and resolve

Locate each finding by state/viewport and selector/region; record consequence, bounded correction, evidence and confidence. Separate confirmed defects, hypotheses and taste. Blocking prevents a key task/access/truthful feedback; major harms comprehension, reading, efficiency or identity; polish is smaller craft work.

For fixes retain expected change and same-state recapture where possible. Recheck affected shared consumers; distinguish resolved/partial/unresolved from checked/failed/untested. Report remaining scoped readability defects as unresolved, not successful delivery. No finding quota.

Read winners' comments: relative preference is not acceptance. Separate input/asset/capture/adapter problems from implementation defects. Distinguish source/native input, palette math/rendering, resize/zoom and screenshots/AT. Report operation/access separately from character/care; scores, compliance, audience and superiority claims need relevant evidence.
