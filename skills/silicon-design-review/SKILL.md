# Silicon design review

Review current revision, direction, constraints and realistic content/growth. State actual fixture, viewport/scale, theme, language and input; screenshots support appearance only.

Review-only work reports findings without editing. Apply authorized fixes and recheck affected states; routine reversible repairs need no new approval sequence.

## Select checks

Inspect order, hierarchy, necessary type, units, status and character. Check reference provenance/task transfer and the reading-model/count rationale. Verify bounded-set capacity at base size and accessible reflow, not tiny text, clipping or needless pagination. A hue/font/pattern is not inherently a defect; allow coherent authorized palette changes.

Trace actual foregrounds and glyphs, including SVG fill/stroke/currentColor, masks/filters and image assets, onto changed surfaces. Inspect crop/bounds and relevant states at use size. An unreadable glyph fails despite unrelated passing swatches; source writes do not prove resolved rendering.

For multiple screens, compare headers/navigation/levels/controls against one shared contract; explain task-based exceptions. A local fix needs no kit/surface. Check supplied growth, bounded widths and quantities/costs/actions together; preserve reading and targets.

Question redundant framing and missed authorized simplification while preserving useful legends, empty states, accessible meanings and safeguards. Fixed-copy/DOM restrictions are input limits, not permission to fake controls. On landings inspect real headline breaks, comparison and action cues; on charts inspect visible units, mark-label relationships and source fidelity.

For affected controls, transitions, overlays or sticky/scroll regions, read [interactive checks](references/interactive-checks.md). Skip for static/screenshot-only work. Full-area activation, deliberate reflow and content-driven heights are not automatic defects. Check matched content/narrow priority of in-scope alternatives; added workflows, persistence and sheets remain conditional.

## Report and resolve

Locate each finding by state/viewport and selector/region; record consequence, bounded correction, evidence and confidence. Separate confirmed defects, hypotheses and taste. Blocking prevents a key task/access/truthful feedback; major harms comprehension, reading, efficiency or identity; polish is smaller craft work.

Retain expected fixes and same-state recaptures where possible; recheck consumers. Separate resolved/partial/unresolved from checked/failed/untested. Remaining readability defects stay unresolved. No finding quota.

Read winners' comments: preference is not acceptance. Separate input/asset/capture/adapter defects from implementation. Distinguish source/native input, palette math/rendering, resize/zoom and screenshots/AT. Separate operation/access from character/care; scores, compliance, audience and superiority claims need evidence.
