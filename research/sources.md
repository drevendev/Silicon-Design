## Primary standards and contextual guidance

- **Web access:** [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is the reference for the web target. The handbook links individual WAI explanations for [text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), [text resizing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html), [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), [target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) and [focus visibility against occlusion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html). Understanding documents explain criteria; the selected checklist is not the full standard.
- **Token interchange:** [DTCG Format 2025.10](https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/format/) describes interchange and token types. It is a Community Group report, not a W3C Recommendation. Our simple palette data does not claim conformance.
- **Games:** [Game Accessibility Guidelines, basic](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/) informs input, readable text, non-color cues and audio independence. Applicability depends on the game and platform; these are contextual guidelines, not a claim of web conformance.

General composition and typography starting values in the handbook are Silicon Design conventions. They are explicitly presented as experiments to validate, not laws attributed to a source. Sources that could not be meaningfully read were not used to support specific rules.

## Reuse policy for this initial version

All handbook prose, skills, examples and checker implementation were authored for this repository. External code, skill bodies, templates, fonts, logos, screenshots, diagrams and harvested discussions are not bundled. External material is linked and its ideas are paraphrased with a stated boundary.

Before a later contribution copies material, inspect the exact file's license and asset rights, retain required notices, and document the transfer. A link in this register does not relicense a source or its trademarks. Silicon Design's MIT license applies to its own original material.

## Owner-critical guidance correction, 2026-11-08

Public [#26](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/26) and its #27–#33 follow-ups specify palette coherence, actual glyph inspection, shared product components, authorized structural simplification, readable density, current-source/growth constraints and the next evaluation's gate. These are owner requirements and failure-class diagnoses, not published private experiment data or fresh preference evidence.

The bundled skill-creator informs self-contained entrypoints, conditional depth and removal of repetitive process. [WAI labeling controls](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/) and the [button pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/button/), read 8 November 2026, support retaining programmatic labels, contextual visible instructions, native button semantics and keyboard/focus behavior when simplifying presentation. [Non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), read the same day, distinguishes meaningful adjacent visual cues and applicable exceptions from unrelated palette calculations. These explanatory resources do not make this handbook a compliance certificate. No source prose or external implementation is copied.

The [implementation record](critical-guidance-implementation-2026-11-08.md) maps changes and preserved requirements to actual authoring/package checks. No new comparative experiment is run by this contribution.
