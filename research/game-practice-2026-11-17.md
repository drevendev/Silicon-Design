# Later game evidence: what to compose, what to leave out

Public owner reports [#52](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/52), [#53](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/53), [#54](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/54) and [#55](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/55), read 17 November 2026. Exact retrieved body hashes are in the [ledger](release-2026-11-17.sources.json). This is a text-only intake and original teaching implementation, not a new product audit. No private source, raw votes or restricted media is published.

## Keep the two BJH studies separate

Both studies used Silicon release 2026.11.15.1, one fresh attempt per method, five real-game surfaces and one owner. Each had 30 ballots. The unchanged original may be recognizable; it is not strictly blind for a familiar owner. Neither repeated single attempts nor differing asset/viewing conditions establish method superiority.

| Study | Silicon screen results: start / combat / shop / reward / result | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #52, no purchased-art instruction | 1–2 / 3–0 / 3–0 / 2–1 / 3–0 | Operational composition, text size, contrast and result formatting were useful. Entry, added deck-size prose, per-offer detail and recovery were criticized. Judged at 800×600, RU, 150% display scale. |
| #53, fullest useful art-library use | 2–1 / 1–2 / 1–2 / 2–1 / 2–1 | Brief-alone won all 15 of its pairs. Motion/hover was named in 10 of 20 commented ballots, contrast in six. Silicon's figure, deck control, track and framed trophy had specific praise, including on lost ballots. Mixed 960×640/800×600, EN/RU, 150% display scale. |

The second study's winner also had layout defects. Minimum-frame hygiene matters for operation; it did not explain the owner's preferences. Art may explain some differences, or differences may reflect chance between single attempts. The corpora were never directly compared and cannot be pooled into a new score. Nobody listened to candidate audio; the consumer's browser suite was not run. These limitations remain even after this release's own fictional checks.

The participant reports show no external reference inspection, mostly retained layout skeletons, extra read-outs and known narrow defects; one art import emitted no file. These observations motivate an early bounded reference decision, an explicit keep/recompose choice when structural work is permitted, verified material intake and effort for meaningful feedback before another sentence. They do not prove these instructions will improve the next generation. The game skill preserves the successful operational hierarchy and contextual semantic colour while making entry, recovery and Inspect actual design scope.

## IdleShop 0.16: later boundaries, not a rewritten result

[#54](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/54) is iterative owner feedback on a published product, not a fresh blind study or proof that Silicon caused the regressions. Earlier #25/#30/#39 work remains credited; #29/#31/#37 retain their limits.

| Status in the source report | Carry forward |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted compact shelves, Market, workers and assortment groups | Goods lead; exact Buy total and real event ledger; grouped time/pay/job/progress/actions; known preferences remain small; familiar signed effects retain their actual calculation and knowledge gates. These arrangements are contextual, not universal templates. |
| Rejected enlarged framed preference controls and spread-out worker relations | Interaction need not inflate visible glyphs; assess occupied task area and both axes, not only overflow. The original control fixture keeps an 18 px glyph in a 44 px native target beside an intentionally oversized counterexample. |
| Accepted compact-layout and credit corrections | Preserve required notices and internal rights records; optional asset/process prose is not mandatory player copy. |
| Future-only Settings simplification, Stock Watch removal, motion and hierarchy/effect changes | Respect the owner's requested scope; do not call those shipped or revive a removed tool because research continues. Binary settings are this product's choice. Interpolated motion must preserve authoritative timing, pause and reduced/off/hidden behavior. |
| Future-only Ware/recipe/empty section/effect-scope repairs | Separate category from peer tags; ingredients → result retains counts and accessible names; no empty Items heading; meaningful disclosure for known entries. Distinguish mechanism/source/scope without rewriting narrow bonuses into global ones. |

The [subtraction guide](../docs/subtraction-and-craft.md), system/game/review workflows and [original controls](../examples/decisions/controls.html) reconcile these cases with existing guidance. No consumer economy or shipped feature is changed here. A meaningful recipe arrow does not reverse Scriptorium's contextual rejection of diagonal link ornaments.

## BJH 0.17: material with stable input and one time model

[#55](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/55) follows integration of the second study's night-road direction. Its reported ordinary tests and incremental browser cases belong to that consumer revision; they were not rerun here and do not establish aesthetic acceptance of every screen. The source distinguishes four unobserved hint cases and untested device/AT/listening/balance qualification.

Portable consequences are narrower than the game's rules: animate artwork within stationary native hit rectangles; feedback follows a committed current encounter and does not replay on reload. Fit the real decision set before adding read-outs, then give exact detail a deliberate Inspect/help layer. Protect the actual action when popups coexist with fixed, sticky or in-flow coaching layouts; an inside-viewport rectangle is insufficient. Reuse one authoritative projection of revision, trigger, capture time and physical remaining uses across compact/full/legacy readers. Return to a mounted physical-copy caller with choice, scroll and focus, not a destroyed popup control.

The new [Nightjar examples and receipts](../examples/decisions/README.md) exercise those failure classes with original synthetic material. They are neither BJH/IdleShop fixes nor new generation samples. Review still reports craft, native input, measured accessibility properties, preference, comprehension and listening separately.
