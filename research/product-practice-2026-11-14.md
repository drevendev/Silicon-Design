Approved art direction coexisted with substantial owner criticism of journey/hierarchy. Do not treat one as acceptance of the other. The garden palette is not a Silicon theme.

Newly authored fictional copy examples for a document desk, not product quotes or observed results:

- Invitation: “Choose a source to prepare a reading copy.”
- Completion: “Reading copy ready. Open copy.” The link must name the exact result.
- Failure: “The reading copy could not be saved. Open retained draft.” Use only when those facts are known. Otherwise retain the actual failure reason and available recovery; do not invent a reassuring cause or a retained draft.

## IdleShop 0.15.1: repeated decisions, precise effects, stable input

[#25](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/25) reports four playtest iterations with one owner and implemented acceptance, with separately reported source/Chromium/WebKit checks. Four rounds are not four independent participants. It reviewed Silicon at `1d0f408016f5761766ab7b0400fd0b03be625b53`; earlier [wireframes](our-practice.md) remain specifications. This contribution does not claim that Silicon generated the game or won a trial.

| Accepted product decision | Portable consequence now incorporated |
| --- | --- |
| Comparable profession and bonus structures | Share grammar/implementation for equivalent meanings; do not equate unrelated numbers |
| Removed repeated captions and oversized empty areas | Subtract framing, not necessary text, targets or workshop character |
| Signed price effects at the selected choice | State the neutral reference and distinguish percentages from probability points |
| Common tier headings; only the applying tier looks active | Explain shared comparison structure once; distinguish possible from applied effects |
| Stock, restock and Remove grouped with the item | Keep actions and exact pool/capacity meaning together; count/capacity may replace a caption |
| Ready/claimable cues follow spendable resources and actual requirements | No accent or alert based on an incomplete condition; preserve non-colour meaning |
| Compact independent-requirement chips | Keep full names and unknowns reachable; remove weaker thresholds only when dependency equivalence is proven, preserving OR/legacy paths |
| Signed aroma gauge with neutral centre | Encode deviation from zero; do not imply half progress at neutral |
| Reserved live-value geometry and stable mode groups | Prevent avoidable jumps; long translations wrap without collisions; equal heights remain contextual |
| Full action body plus independent child controls | Test label/icon/padding and keyboard activation without nesting arbitrary controls or double dispatch |
| Authoritative cycle and caravan motion | Interpolate actual state; pause/rebase on cycle/visibility changes; keep static timing; never advance game rules through animation |
| Global rules in Help; specific help near decisions | Keep hover/focus/tap access and useful flavor; remove repetitive implementation prose |

Keep inventory, shelf, reserved and free stock distinct. Accepted rewards differ from offered/discarded rewards; zero projected wages do not prove no assignments. Current/next/historical/rate/estimate wording must follow its real source. No formatting or help change may alter economy, RNG, saved quotes, knowledge or unlocks.

These decisions inform [games](../docs/products/games.md), [applications](../docs/products/apps.md), the skills and [craft procedure](../docs/subtraction-and-craft.md). Native devices, manual AT, broader comprehension, long-term attachment and better future generations remain unverified. Later unmerged prototypes are not adopted production design. Separate asynchronous and localization specimens remain [#6](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/6) and [#7](https://github.com/drevendev/Silicon-Design/issues/7).
