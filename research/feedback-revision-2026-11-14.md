## What changed

- All five portable skills carry the relevant subtraction/object/precision decisions without requiring the handbook, another skill or a new framework. Discovery descriptions and conditional interaction-review routing are preserved.
- Game/build/review now require a final same-state craft comparison and correction within the available budget. Record removals or deliberate retentions; technical PASS, craft judgment, preference and explicit owner acceptance are separate.
- Duplicate labels, badges, selected-mode captions and static help are questioned when count, meter, position or selection already carries the meaning. Visible instructions, names/context, legends, deficits, lock reasons and safeguards survive.
- Name the dominant object; prefer one enclosing boundary, justify additional functional grouping, align corner actions and inspect both glyph and hit target. Uniform rows can share dividers. No universal one-border, list, centring, hue or style ban is introduced.
- Exact action totals stay visible at commitment, including large amounts. Secondary precision may use reachable detail. Inspect long translated names at minimum supported width.
- Reference inspection must actually show the relevant task UI before composition, or its absence must be disclosed then. Article headers and unviewed URLs cannot clear the step. Reuse prior suitable inspection for tiny fixes; unavailable access does not create an indefinite block or extra approval.
- Report task-relevant shared defects encountered on the path even when editing them is outside scope.
- [Product practice](product-practice-2026-11-14.md) incorporates the outstanding Compost Club and owner-playtested IdleShop contributions into existing app/game guidance: scope of asynchronous work, discoverable outcomes, exact source material, applied versus possible effects, resource pools, signed gauges, live geometry and authoritative motion.

The [craft procedure](../docs/subtraction-and-craft.md), workflow, layout guidance, checklist and templates agree with the entrypoints. Detailed examples remain optional; there is no extra always-loaded reference.
