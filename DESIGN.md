# The Silicon Design handbook

## Purpose and audience

Help our developers and coding agents make specific, reviewable design decisions for websites, apps and games. The first useful action is adopting one project brief and one representative flow.

The public promise is to give competent AI-made interfaces a character people can care about. The opening should state that problem clearly, name the actual principles/skills, and provide a short route into the work. “Парадная” is the Russian introduction and sits after the primary handbook routes.

## Repository experience

The README explains the outcome, gives a short adoption path, routes to five skills and links visible teaching examples. Detailed foundations and research stay behind explicit links. English is the maintained handbook language; README.ru.md is a concise Russian entry point.

Avoid invented maturity badges, ratings and efficacy claims. Source ideas need boundaries and revisions. Fictional examples remain clearly labelled.

## Visual specimens

The gallery wrapper is a quiet switcher: system sans, visible links and one active world at a time. Query URLs identify each example; the inner experience owns its composition, imagery, controls and voice. Switching retains local state. The wrapper is not an identity to impose on consuming projects.

Field Notes retains three reading models for one photograph and fixture: street atlas, contact sheet and continuous reading. Their shared field book extends to a second surface. Dispatch connects a stable bench, readable work order and handover records with undo. Mosslight connects a painted world, making book and journal with a painted completed state. Each has a filled brief; wide and narrow compositions are inspected separately. Dispatch/Mosslight switching retains state on their shared page; the separate Field Notes visit has its own local book.

The [original critique](research/specimen-critique.md) and [revision review](examples/REVIEW.md) show why the experiences changed. The resulting local prototypes demonstrate decisions, not proven attachment, production readiness or a universal design benchmark.

[Issue #8 application](research/issue-8-application.md) makes the output contracts concrete. [Anti Slop](research/benchmark.md) is the first comparative target; the protocol requires equal context and retained matched runs before claiming superiority.
