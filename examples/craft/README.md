Serve the repository locally (`python -m http.server --bind 127.0.0.1`) for JSON-backed charts and downloads. Each baseline intentionally preserves its failures; corrected pages stay separate and share fixed data, states, fonts, languages and viewports.

Use `?lang=ru` on workshop pages for long Russian names. `?state=blocked` retains all six offers with unavailable reasons; `?state=empty` shows a meaningful empty state. Zero stock and unknown stock/price are different. Both original and correction reset on reload and have identical purchasing/unassignment capabilities. Teaching controls expose a stock change and repeated draw; they are explicitly outside the product flow. For stale confirmation, open Review, use `workshopQA.setStock(0,1)` in browser developer tools, then Confirm. No transaction is accepted.

The six independent bench objects use a grid for recognition/comparison. The fixed 1280 px layout has 1,160 usable main-content pixels after 60 px side margins; three columns receive about 377 px each after two 13 px gutters. At 320 px the usable width is 288 px and objects reflow to one column. Natural heights and vertical scrolling are deliberate. This case does not promise six offers fit every viewport; the existing [nine-offer capacity fixture](../capacity.html) retains its separate 1080×800 contract and growth case. No text/offer is hidden to claim capacity.

Relevant task UI inspected before composition: the existing capacity purchase/detail fixture and the preserved Scriptorium original plus both generated sites, with provenance separated in the [release record](../../research/release-2026-11-15.md). Relationships transferred were price/effect proximity, early real material and task-led grouping. No private code, screenshots or assets are included here.

Author judgment: removal of duplicate captions/inner boxes makes the product and purchase decision earlier and quieter; the original useful background and alignment remain. A price modifier remains visually attached to its price. Necessary labels, deficits, unknown/zero distinctions, explanations and actions survive. The long-name first card has a different natural height; forcing it to match shorter cards would add empty space or conceal content. The link example’s headline is deliberately subordinate to its actual samples/action. These are bounded editorial judgments, not a universal grid/card/centering/font/color recipe or a new preference result.

Keyboard, rendering and source checks are reported separately from craft and owner acceptance. Actual browser zoom, physical touch/controller hardware, assistive technology, forced colors, learning and future-agent behavior remain unverified. No public Scriptorium variant or real-game adoption is delivered by these fictional cases.
