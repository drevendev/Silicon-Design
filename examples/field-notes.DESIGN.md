# Field Notes — fictional publication

## Context

A reader is choosing an essay about urban observation on a phone or laptop. The specimen is a fictional publication front page. Its local links expose an excerpt, not a real archive or newsletter service.

## Direction

An observant, unhurried journal: a large serif title, a narrow argument-shaped text block, a strong chapter number and thin editorial rules. Navigation and metadata use system sans. The first attention is the essay's subject; the next step is reading an excerpt.

A dense thumbnail grid was considered, but would emphasize browsing volume rather than the selected story. The chosen direction gives up simultaneous visibility of many articles. Its identity comes from composition and editorial voice as well as color.

## System and behavior

- Georgia with serif fallback for display; system sans for functional copy. Real typography changes across platforms, so the role matters more than exact glyph matching.
- Light paper-like surface `#f3eee4`, ink `#232b29`, supporting text `#535e58`, editorial accent `#8b3426`.
- Reading text 1.0625rem with generous line height; a bounded fluid display and wrapping heading. A chapter number anchors the margin on wide screens and sits above content on narrow screens.
- No animation, remote image or custom font is required. Native disclosure holds the excerpt; links identify their local destination.
- Include a long title, 320 CSS px width and text enlargement in review. No dark theme is supplied in this specimen.
