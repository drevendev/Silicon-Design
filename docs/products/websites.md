# Websites

Choose the visitor's journey before a hero pattern. Marketing needs comprehension and evidence; a publication needs a readable route into articles; a portfolio needs work the visitor can assess.

## Compose the real content

Identify the product/subject, intended visitor and useful next step. Show a representative result when it clarifies the promise. Order sections by the questions they answer: comparable facts, a process or a sustained argument need different structures.

Inspect the actual headline block, navigation and main actions together at target size. Test the available face and real line breaks; a larger title alone does not establish character. Group comparable choices so their relationship is apparent. In a route selector, for example, distinguish selectable routes from a tariff summary instead of accidentally making them look like four equivalent choices.

Use type, imagery, layout and voice together. A distinctive crop or explanatory illustration may do more than repeated backgrounds. Let changing density follow the story, not arbitrary alternation.

## Make the action credible

Use labels and useful cues to show destination and consequence. Preserve distinctions between navigation, disclosure, selection and action where those behaviours exist. An ordinary link does not need a fake dropdown arrow. A prototype action must not imply a working integration.

Use available brand material honestly. Do not fabricate testimonials, customer logos, awards, usage measurements or proprietary assets. If supplied copy is questionable, distinguish the input issue and propose editing only when changing the copy is allowed.

## Verify the requested unit

For a single first viewport, inspect that whole frame without adding downstream sections or an invented conversion flow. For a full site or journey, inspect the relevant lower content and action result too. A reading site may need a long article, links, lists and figures when those are in scope.

Check relevant narrow/wide layouts, real headline wrapping, comparable groups, loading/fallback and navigation. Verify actual activation where implemented; a static screenshot does not prove a dropdown works. Set asset/performance budgets from deployment context, not decorative habits.

Choose a specific feeling and subject-grounded idea and keep it coherent across requested surfaces. Review [character](../character.md) separately from navigation, readability and loading. A quiet working layer can coexist with a distinctive opening.
